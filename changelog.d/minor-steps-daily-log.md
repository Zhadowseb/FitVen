### Added
- **One number a day is kept, on the phone and in the cloud.** The day's steps - and which source they came from - are written to `Daily_Steps` on the phone and to the new `public.daily_steps` in Supabase (one row for each person and day, owner-only). The screens read that table, so a day stays when the health app is switched off or has forgotten it, and a new phone gets its history back from the account. `DailyStepsSync` does it on launch and on every return to the foreground (at most every five minutes): the last three days are read into the table, the history the first time a source answers, and the phone and the cloud meet.
- **The sources are layered, best first, and never added together:** the phone's health app (Apple Health, Health Connect), then the phone's own motion history (iPhone, about 7 days back), and later FitVen's own step sensor on Android. A day takes the best source that has a number for it; a worse source never replaces a better one - not when the better one stops answering, and not when two phones meet in the cloud - and between two numbers from the same source the larger wins, since a day only grows. There are no clocks in that rule, so the order the phones speak in does not matter.
- **Nothing is read until Allow has been tapped on the steps card or page.** A motion permission given for a walk is not a yes to the whole day, so the daily steps wait for their own yes (kept in the person's own database), and `NSMotionUsageDescription` now names the daily steps too. A source's history is read once, the first time that source answers - not "when the table is empty", so Apple Health turned on after the motion history, or on a new phone that got its days from the cloud first, still gets its history. Android asks for 29 days back, inside what Health Connect shows without the history permission.
- **On an iPhone without Apple Health,** the steps come from the phone's own motion history instead: when a week has no number after Apple Health has been asked, the motion permission is asked once, and only then. Nothing is read behind anybody's back.
- **The migration `20261011090000_daily-steps.sql` has to be run in the Supabase SQL editor.** Until it has, the days stay on the phone, nothing breaks and the next start tries again. The table needs the privacy policy to have been accepted for a write, refuses anyone but the owner (followers included) and goes with the account.

### Changed
- **A day the phone has no steps for is no longer a day of zero steps:** it has no number, and is left out of the averages. Today is the exception, once a source has been answering this week.
- **The privacy policy now says the steps are stored** - one number a day, on the phone and in the account, only readable by the owner - and its version is raised again, so everybody is asked to accept it once more; `web/privacy/index.html` is rebuilt. The upload waits for the policy this build carries to have been accepted.
- Android still needs Health Connect for steps (Android 9+ with a source app writing to it); the layer that counts with the phone's own sensor, so nobody has to install anything, comes next.
