### Added
- **Ask FitVen: an AI you can ask about your own training** ("Why is my bench stuck?", "What should I train today?"). A bar on Home, under your split, opens a chat that reads your own workouts, records, program and daily steps - never anybody else's, and never your sickness entries - and answers with text, a chart of your own numbers and, when it has one, a suggested change you can add to your next workout (and undo). It suggests; it writes nothing itself.
  - **Three kinds of answer, a monthly quota each:** Haiku 10, Sonnet 5 and Opus 2 questions a month. Before a question is sent, FitVen first says which of them it recommends for it (a cheap check that sees none of your data and does not use up a question); you can choose another.
  - **Only your data, by construction.** The AI never sends SQL or names a table: it calls eight fixed read functions in a database schema of its own (`ai`), and each one filters on the signed-in person itself, on every table it reads, as well as row level security - the follower policy would otherwise have let it read a friend's workouts. `scripts/ask-fitven-isolation.sql` proves it on the real project (read-only, rolls back); `scripts/test-ask-fitven-sql.js` runs the migration against copies of the tables with a follower and fails if a function breaks a rule.
  - **A yes first** (one screen, and a switch under Settings → Privacy); the conversation stays on the phone; nothing about a question or an answer is logged.
  - **Privacy policy:** says what is sent, to whom (Anthropic) and what is kept (that you said yes and your monthly count); the version is raised, so everybody is asked to accept it again.
- **To switch it on:** run `supabase/migrations/20261026090000_ask-fitven.sql`, add `ai` to the exposed schemas (Settings → API), set the secret `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, deploy `ask-fitven`, and run `scripts/ask-fitven-isolation.sql` once. Until then the bar says the assistant is not available. The full design is `docs/ASK-FITVEN.md`.
