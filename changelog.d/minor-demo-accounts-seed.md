### Added
- **Demo accounts: a made-up social circle for the content captured from the app.** `scripts/demo/seed-demo.js` (`npm run demo:plan | demo:probe | demo:apply | demo:status | demo:reset`) creates five made-up people - Alex Morgan, the account the emulator signs in as, and four friends - with confirmed sign-ups on `<name>.demo@fitven.dk` addresses (no mail is sent), profiles, photos, recorded consent, 13 follows between them, and seven finished workouts with sets, a feed post for each and 14 likes. The cast, the follows and the workouts are in `scripts/demo/cast.js`; the post payload has the shape `socialPostService` builds.
  - It is run by hand on your machine with the service role key from `.env` and by no workflow; nothing in it prints or sends the key. `apply` and `reset` only print a plan without `--yes`, and `demo:probe` first checks the cloud's own description of `workout_type_instance`, `exercise_instance`, `set`, `social_post` and `social_post_like` against what the seed writes, and writes nothing if a column is missing or required and unfilled - those tables are not described in the repository's migrations.
  - **What keeps it from touching anything real:** it only writes to the project in `supaBaseClient.js`; a user is a demo user only with both the `fitven_demo` marker and a `.demo@fitven.dk` address; every follow is between the five; posts are `following` visibility; the workouts have no centre, so none reaches a public leaderboard; and the finished workouts are not live, so none raises a start notification. Reset removes every demo account and what it owns. Photos for three of the five are cut from the store screenshots (`scripts/demo/avatars/`) and are soft; larger ones can replace them.
  - Alex's own history is not seeded: a workout has to be in the app's own local database to count, so it comes from using the app on the account. `docs/DEMO-ACCOUNTS.md` has the rest.
- **Tests:** `scripts/test-demo-seed.js` - the cast and its follows are consistent, a workout and its post are built like the app's own (top set is the best by weight times reps, the record is the top set, unique `sync_id`s), only a user with the marker and the address counts as demo, the project URL is checked, and `plan` and an unconfirmed `apply` or `reset` touch nothing and need no key.
