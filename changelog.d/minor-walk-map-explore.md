### Changed
- **A finished walk's map can be explored.** Open a finished walk and the map shows the whole route at once, can be dragged and zoomed (with the +/- buttons on Android), and is taller. The orange "you are here" dot is gone from it - a ring marks where the route ended - and the camera no longer follows. A walk that is running keeps the map as it was: it follows you and is not dragged.
