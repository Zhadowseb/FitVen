### Changed
- **A splash instead of a line of grey text while the saved session is restored.** A barbell loads three plates a side one by one, lifts with a small shake, lands with a bump and starts over; under it the title, a line that cycles through "Warming up", "Loading the plates", "Chalking up" and "Spotting your session", and a thin progress bar sweeping at the bottom. It is `src/Resources/Components/RestoringSession/` (from the design handoff) and is shown in both places `App.js` waited for the session: while the login is being read, and while the login, the stored accent and the language are. It is shown for as long as the app really waits and on no timer.
  - The text is in the translation files, in English and Danish (`common.restoringSession.*`); the first of the two places had the English text written straight into `App.js`. The colours come from the theme, inline, so it follows light and dark, and the stored accent once that has been read; the style file has layout only.
  - Only `Animated` and `View`, on the native driver, no new package. With "Reduce motion" it stops on a still frame and the line does not cycle. It is a progress bar to a screen reader with the title as its label, and the barbell is hidden from it.
- **Tests:** `scripts/test-restoring-session.js` checks that it is what both places show for the real loading flags, with no timer, that its text is in both languages, that it has no colour of its own and handles Reduce motion and screen readers.
